> **Important Note**: This is the guide on contribution to the <u>frontend</u> codebase, for contribution towards site <u>backend</u>, please see Backend Contribution Guide

# Contribution Guide

We're accepting help from anyone! This codebase is kept open source so anyone may contribute to it, use it as a reference and/or learn from it!

## Found a bug, or have a suggestion?

If you find a <u>bug</u>, or are here to report a <u>site problem</u> of any kind, you can open an [issue](https://github.com/RGSS-CS/williams-rgss-website-dev-frontend/issues) on this repo.

For <u>security vulnerabilities</u>, please email [security@rgsscs.org](mailto:security@rgsscs.org) instead of openning an issue.

If you have a suggestion of a new feature or possible enhanced to this project in any way, feel free to let us know by creating an [issue](https://github.com/RGSS-CS/williams-rgss-website-dev-frontend/issues), making sure to select the "enhancement" label!

## Want to contribute to the code?

You can make a [fork](https://github.com/RGSS-CS/williams-rgss-website-dev-frontend/fork) of our repository, where you can make your own changes (if you are looking for somewhere to start, take a look at our [open issues](https://github.com/RGSS-CS/williams-rgss-website-dev-frontend/issues)).

Then, you can open a [pull request](https://github.com/RGSS-CS/williams-rgss-website-dev-frontend/pulls) to merge your changes into our codebase, but will have to be reviewed by a codebase/site maintainer first.

## Star this project!

If you liked this project or contributing to it, you can star this project and share it with others! Stars motivate maintainers and help to keep our projects alive!
